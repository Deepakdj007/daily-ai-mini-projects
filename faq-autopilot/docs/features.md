# CloudNest Features

## Deployments

Push to your connected Git branch and CloudNest builds and deploys automatically. Every deploy gets a unique preview URL.

## Rollbacks

Any past deploy can be restored with one click. Rollbacks are instant and do not require a rebuild.

## Environments

Each project supports three environments: development, staging, and production. Environment variables are encrypted at rest.

## Integrations

CloudNest integrates with GitHub, GitLab, and Bitbucket. Slack notifications are available on the Team and Business plans.

## Regions

Apps can be deployed to data centres in Mumbai, Singapore, and Frankfurt. The region is chosen per project and cannot be changed after creation.
