# CloudNest Pricing

## Plans

CloudNest has three plans: Starter, Team, and Business.

- **Starter** — ₹0/month. For solo developers. 1 project, 2 GB storage.
- **Team** — ₹2,999/month. For small teams. 10 projects, 100 GB storage, up to 5 seats.
- **Business** — ₹4,999/month. For growing companies. Unlimited projects, 1 TB storage, up to 25 seats.

## Billing cycle

Paid plans are billed monthly by default. Annual billing is available and gives you two months free (pay for 10, get 12).

## Refund policy

We offer a 60-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. Cancel within 60 days of your first payment for a full refund, no questions asked.

## Taxes

Prices shown exclude GST. Indian customers are charged 18% GST at checkout.
