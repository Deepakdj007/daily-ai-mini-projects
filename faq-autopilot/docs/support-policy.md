# CloudNest Support Policy

## Support channels

Starter users get community forum support. Team and Business users get email support. Business users also get a private Slack channel.

## Response times

We aim to respond to email support within 24 business hours. Business plan customers get priority responses within 4 business hours.

## Support hours

Support operates Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm IST. We are closed on Indian national holidays.

## Escalation

If an issue is not resolved within 3 business days, it is automatically escalated to a senior engineer.
