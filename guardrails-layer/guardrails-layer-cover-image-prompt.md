# Cover Image — Prompts

Cinematic cover for the guardrails-layer post. Three figures in one line:
an archer attacking, a divine shield standing between, a machine holding the
ground, and behind it the people it serves. Landscape, no text.

The chain of protection is the whole idea — **the shield guards the machine, and
the machine guards the people.**

---

## Variant A — Wide standoff (recommended)

Left to right the frame reads: attacker, shield, robot, the protected. One
glance and the hierarchy is obvious.

```
Cinematic film still, wide landscape shot, low camera angle. Three planes of action arranged left to right across a vast plain of cracked earth at dusk.

Far left, in near silhouette against a blazing horizon: a lone human archer, barefoot and wrapped in torn cloth, drawing an enormous ancient bow of blackened gold. The drawn arrow burns with a contained white-gold light that throws hard shadows across the ground and catches the tendons of the archer's arms. Two arrows already loosed streak across the frame as thin incandescent lines.

Centre of frame, planted upright in the earth and dominating the composition: a colossal circular shield of weathered bronze, its face covered in deep engraved geometric patterns, cracked and pitted from earlier impacts. Where the arrows strike it the metal blooms molten white, and the light bleeds outward through the engraved channels like veins. Fragments of shattered arrows fall at its base.

Right of the shield, standing firm in its shadow: a tall humanoid robot of matte brushed steel and exposed cabling, weathered and scarred, both forearms raised carrying smaller angular shields, feet braced wide. It is not flinching.

Behind the robot, closest to camera-right and softly out of focus: a small cluster of ordinary people sheltering - a woman holding a child against her shoulder, an old man with a hand on the robot's leg, a young man looking back over his shoulder. They are lit only by the warm spill of light that gets past the shield.

Lighting: hard low sun directly behind the archer, blowing the left third of the frame into pale gold and reducing the archer to a black shape. The shield's impact glow is the second light source, casting cold white rim light along the robot's frame and warm bounce onto the faces behind it. Deep blue shadow pools in the foreground.

Atmosphere: fine dust and ash suspended in the backlight, long shadows stretching toward camera.

Shot on 35mm anamorphic, deep focus on the shield and robot, archer and background softly compressed, natural lens flare, film grain, subtle halation on the hot highlights.

Colour palette: molten gold and white against deep slate blue and charcoal. Desaturated, high contrast, no other colours.

Mood: mythic, weighted, a siege held. Ancient conflict rendered at scale.

Photorealistic, epic cinematography, highly detailed metal and fabric texture, in the style of Denis Villeneuve and Roger Deakins.

--ar 16:9 --style raw --v 7
```

## Variant B — Behind the shield

Camera sits with the defenders. The archer is distant and small, the shield
fills the frame, and the human faces carry the emotion.

```
Cinematic film still, wide landscape shot from behind the defenders, looking past them toward the attacker.

Dominating the left and centre of frame, seen from its sheltered side: the inner face of a colossal bronze shield, its surface ribbed and buttressed, glowing white-hot in patches where arrows strike the far side, the light bleeding through cracks and engraved channels in thin brilliant lines.

Standing directly behind it with its back to camera: a tall weathered humanoid robot of matte steel, arms raised and braced against the shield's inner rim, shoulders set. Sparks and molten fragments rain down around it.

Behind the robot in the foreground, faces lit by the shield's glow: a small group of ordinary people crouched low - a woman shielding a child's head, an elderly man with his eyes closed, a teenager staring up at the light with an expression between fear and awe. Their faces are the emotional centre of the frame.

Far in the background through a gap at the shield's edge, small and sharp against a blazing horizon: the silhouette of the archer, bow drawn, an arrow of white fire nocked and about to release.

Lighting: the shield's impact glow is the key light, cold brilliant white washing over the robot's back and the upturned faces. A distant warm sunset provides the only other source, silhouetting the archer. Everything else falls to deep blue-black.

Shot on 40mm, shallow depth of field with the human faces sharp and the shield surface falling soft, dust and embers drifting through the light, natural film grain.

Colour palette: brilliant white and cold blue against warm amber horizon and deep charcoal shadow.

Mood: sheltered, tense, held. The moment before the next impact.

Photorealistic, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, highly detailed faces and metal texture, epic cinematography.

--ar 16:9 --style raw --v 7
```

## Variant C — The instant of impact

Tightest and most graphic. Almost abstract, strongest as a thumbnail.

```
Cinematic film still, wide landscape crop, the exact instant of impact.

Centre frame: an arrow of white-gold fire striking the face of a colossal engraved bronze shield. The point of contact is a blinding bloom of molten light, the shield's metal glowing through from within, shockwave rings of dust blasting outward, fragments of the arrow shattering and spinning away as burning shards.

Left edge, small and distant and almost lost in the glare: the black silhouette of an archer, bow still extended from the release.

Right edge, mostly in shadow and cut by the frame: the shoulder and raised forearm of a large weathered steel robot, braced against the shield, rimmed in hard white light from the impact. Further right and heavily out of focus, the suggestion of human figures crouched low, only their edges catching the glow.

Lighting: the impact is the sole light source, a single hard point throwing violent radial shadows across the cracked ground in every direction. Everything beyond its reach is deep blue-black.

Shot on 85mm, very shallow depth of field, extreme dynamic range, motion blur on the flying fragments, heavy natural lens flare, film grain, chromatic aberration at the frame edges.

Colour palette: molten white-gold at the centre falling off to iron grey and blue-black. Almost monochrome outside the blast.

Mood: violent, decisive, absorbed. The hit lands and stops.

Photorealistic, hyper-detailed, high speed cinematography, dramatic single-source lighting.

--ar 16:9 --style raw --v 7
```

## Why these are built this way

**The shield is ancient bronze, not an energy barrier.** Divine reads as
engraved metal and contained fire, not as a glowing force field — a force field
dates the image instantly and looks like every other AI safety graphic.

**Three planes, one line.** Archer, shield, robot, people. The eye travels left
to right and picks up the whole hierarchy without a caption: something attacks,
something absorbs it, something holds, someone is kept safe.

**The robot is scarred and braced, not gleaming.** A pristine chrome robot reads
as a product render. A weathered one reads as something that has been standing
there a while.

**The people behind are ordinary and specific.** A woman with a child, an old
man, a teenager. Generic crowds carry no feeling; three specific people do.

**Two light sources only** — the sun behind the archer and the glow at the point
of impact. That is what makes it read as cinematography instead of an
illustration.

Variant A carries the most story at full carousel width. Variant B puts human
faces at the centre if you want warmth over scale. Variant C survives being
shrunk to a thumbnail.
