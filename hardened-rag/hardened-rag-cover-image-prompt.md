# hardened-rag — cover image prompt

One metaphor: a document that looks exactly like the real thing, held up
against the light, and only then showing what it is.

## Primary

Extreme close-up, cinematic. A woman archivist in her thirties holds a single
sheet of paper up against a bare window, backlit hard so the page glows and the
printed text reverses through it. Her eyes are narrowed in the moment of
recognition. Behind the glowing sheet, a second sheet is visible through it,
misaligned by a few millimetres, its text almost but not quite matching. Dust in
the shaft of light. Deep shadow on the room side of her face, warm daylight rim
on the other. Muted archive-room palette, oxidised paper, dark wood. Shot on
85mm, shallow depth of field, natural light only. Photographic, no text legible.

`--ar 16:9 --style raw --v 7`

## Alternate

A pair of hands in a dim reading room, laying one document precisely on top of
another on a lightbox. The lower sheet shines through the upper one; the two
are identical in layout and differ in a single line. The only light in the frame
comes from the box beneath, throwing the hands and the underside of the face
into hard relief. Everything beyond the table falls to black. Cool clinical
light against warm paper. Photographic, cinematic, no legible text.

`--ar 16:9 --style raw --v 7`

## Why this and not the obvious thing

The subject is not security and not data. It is the moment someone realises the
convincing document is the forged one. Avoid: shields, locks, glowing networks,
floating cards, holograms, code on screens, anything neon.
