# second-read — cover image prompt

## The idea

A radiologist at a reading station, caught in the half-second *after* the X-ray has gone dark. The report is already written. The film is no longer there. The metaphor is the whole project: a confident reading made with nothing to read.

## Midjourney / Flux / DALL·E prompt

> Extreme close-up, cinematic, of a radiologist's face in a darkened reading room, lit only from the front by a large lightbox that has just switched off — the light dying on their skin, the last cold blue glow draining from one side of the face while the other falls into deep shadow. Their eyes are still fixed on where the film was, pupils wide, mouth slightly open mid-sentence. A faint rectangular reflection of the now-blank panel still sits in the surface of their eye. Shot on 85mm, shallow depth of field, the background dissolving into black. Muted clinical palette — cold blue-grey light, warm skin, no other colour. Film grain, natural skin texture, no retouching. Documentary stillness, not drama. --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 7

## Variant, if a face is not wanted

> Extreme close-up of a pair of hands resting on a reading-room desk in the dark, palms up and empty, lit from the front by a lightbox that has just gone out — the last cold blue light sliding off the fingertips into shadow. A radiology report lies face-down beside them, already signed. Shot on 85mm, shallow depth of field, deep black background. Cold blue-grey and warm skin tones only. Film grain, visible skin texture, documentary stillness. --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 7

## Why this one

The light does the work: it is leaving. The reader is still speaking. Nothing in the frame is a diagram, a hologram, a floating UI panel or a glowing network — the image has to carry "answered without looking" on a human face alone, because that is what the project measures.
